Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy covers Ronin Receipts, an internal tool operated by Ronin Software LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company ("we", "us"). Ronin Receipts is used only by us to manage the books of Ronin Software LLC and Ronin Technologies, LLC. It is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

What the tool accesses

When connected to a QuickBooks Online company we own, Ronin Receipts reads purchase and expense transactions (date, amount, vendor, payment account) and uploads receipt files that we already received by email, attaching them to the matching transaction. It does not create, change or delete transactions.

Where data is stored

Receipt files and a matching log are kept in our own Google Workspace account. QuickBooks access tokens are kept encrypted in the macOS Keychain on a single computer we control. Nothing is stored on third party servers other than Intuit and Google.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone. Our outside bookkeeper and CPA may see the receipts in QuickBooks as part of normal bookkeeping.

Retention and disconnecting

Receipts are kept as long as required for tax records. Disconnecting the app in QuickBooks immediately revokes its access, and we then delete the stored tokens.
